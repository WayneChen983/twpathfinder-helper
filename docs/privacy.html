Who collects your data

WayneChen983, the author of this project. This is not an official tool and is not affiliated with the Youth Development Administration, Ministry of Education.

What is collected

The name, mobile number, email address, birth year, education, experience and background you enter in the sign-up form, and the application draft you choose to upload.

How it is used

  1. To contact you and provide application-related help and notices.
  2. To understand users' backgrounds in order to improve this tool and its writing knowledge base.
  3. To train and improve AI models. This includes the content of the application draft you upload.

If any new use is added, this notice will be updated first and people who have already signed up will be asked for consent again.

Where it is stored and for how long

In a Google Sheets spreadsheet and a Google Drive folder under the author's Google account. It is kept until you ask for it to be deleted.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by emailing tw466376@gmail.com. Deletion removes your row in the spreadsheet, your uploaded file, and your content from any training data that has been prepared. Content that has already been used to train a model cannot be taken back out of that model.

If you choose not to provide your data

You cannot complete the sign-up. You can still use the tool directly from GitHub without signing up.

If you are under 18

Your parent or legal guardian must read and agree to this notice before you submit the form.
